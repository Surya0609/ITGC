ITGC Evidence Analyzer

Welcome to the Analyzer Workspace

This tool analyzes configuration evidence and validates Information Technology General Controls (ITGC). It automatically maps system parameters against standard baselines to output comprehensive risk matrices and compliance reports.

Non-Intrusive: This tool only parses output files. It does not modify systems, nor does it require an active connection to your servers.
